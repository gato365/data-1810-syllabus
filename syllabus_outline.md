## Syllabus Organization

1. **Instructor Contact Information**

   * Contact details
   * Office location
   * Office and learning hours
   * Preferred method of communication

2. **General Course Information**

   * Course title and section
   * Class meeting days, times, and location
   * Prerequisites, if applicable

3. **Purpose of the Course**

   * Course description
   * Role of the course within the program

4. **Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)**

   * Present the outcomes as clear, concise bullet points

5. **Course Materials**

   * Required or recommended textbook
   * Software, technology, and other course materials

6. **Assignment and Grade Breakdown**

   * Assignment categories
   * Point values
   * Percentage of the final grade

7. **Assignment Descriptions and Expectations**

   * Purpose and requirements of each assignment
   * Due dates and submission procedures
   * Policies regarding deadlines, late work, and missing assignments

8. **Course Timeline and Assignment Schedule**

   * Weekly topics
   * Assignment and assessment dates
   * Important course deadlines

9. **Course Policies**

   * Attendance and missed check-ins
   * Late and missing work
   * Artificial intelligence use
   * Academic integrity
   * Diversity, equity, inclusion, and classroom respect
   * Guidelines for succeeding in the course
   * Course-related trips and special events
   * Stickers or other course incentives
   * Disability Resource Center (DRC) accommodations
